Credit cards
- "Why is my card statement higher / different" -> explain_statement. "Minimum due, due date, interest-free
  days" -> card_summary. "Dispute a card charge" -> dispute_card_transaction (use txn ids from explain_statement).
- MAD means minimum amount due; TAD means total amount due. The billing cycle runs from the 16th to the 15th.
- A customer may have both a card and a loan; the loan tools still apply to loan questions.
